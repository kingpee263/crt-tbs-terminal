Execution Rules
1 · HTF Range (C1)

Closed HTF candle with body ≥ 50% of total range. Defines CRTH, 50% Equilibrium, and CRTL.

2 · HTF Key Level

Range extreme must engage an Order Block, Fair Value Gap, or Old High/Low liquidity pool.

3 · Session / SMT

Purge occurs inside the manipulation window for FX/Metals; synthetic indices trade 24/7 on structure.

4 · Turtle Soup / TBS

LTF candle sweeps and closes beyond CRTH/CRTL. 2–6 inside bars prior to sweep increases compression grade.

5 · Model #1 Trigger

Enter on LTF candle body close back through the Model #1 reference candle before price reaches 50% EQ.

6 · Stop Placement

Placed strictly beyond the manipulation extreme wick plus ATR buffer.

7 · 50% Equilibrium Rule

Bank 50% at the CRT midpoint (TP1) and move stop loss to breakeven; hold remainder for opposing CRT extreme (TP2).

8 · Pair Quant Filter

Online logistic regression tracks each symbol's historical sweep depth and TP1 hit rate to filter out low-expectancy setups.
