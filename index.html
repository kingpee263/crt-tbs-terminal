The unified strategy (what the engine checks)
1 · HTF CRT candle

Closed candle with body ≥ 50% of its range and larger than each wick. Mark CRTH, 50%, CRTL. PDF 1 p.7–8

2 · Key level

CRT must trade into a real OB, FVG, or purge an old high/low (OHP/OLP). PDF 1 p.9–14

3 · Time + SMT

Sweep inside the AMD manipulation window; correlated pair fails to sweep. Off for synthetics. PDF 2 p.12–14, 24–28

4 · TBS

First LTF candle that closes beyond CRTH/CRTL. 2–6 inside candles before it = A+. PDF 1 p.16–17

5 · Model #1 entry

Enter when an LTF candle closes beyond the Model #1 candle. Invalid if the HTF candle closes beyond the level or price reaches 50% first. PDF 1 p.18–20, PDF 2 p.18

6 · Stop loss

Beyond the manipulation extreme + 0.1 ATR (+1 ATR against Boom/Crash/GainX/PainX spikes).

7 · 50% rule

Close half at 50% of the CRT and move SL to breakeven; runner to the opposite end. Skip if RR to 50% < 1. PDF 1 p.8, 29 · PDF 2 p.16

8 · OTE / KOD

Missed Model #1? Use the 50–79% OTE of the displacement leg, or the KOD sweep after a close past 50%. PDF 2 p.18–22

Synthetic indices — what changes

Deriv and Weltrade synthetics are generated by an algorithm 24/7. There are no sessions and no correlated assets, so Time and SMT are switched off for them and are not counted in the grade. Candle-geometry rules (CRT, key levels, TBS, Model #1, 50% rule) still run. Whether they have an edge on a given synthetic is something only the Backtest tab can tell you.
